## 2. Step-by-Step: How to Index & Rank on Google Search Console (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Open Google Search Console
1. Navigate to: **[https://search.google.com/search-console/](https://search.google.com/search-console/)**
2. Sign in with your official Google Account (`pavankcet@gmail.com` or `hrlinternationalprivatelimited@gmail.com`).

### Step 2: Add Your Production Property
1. Click **"Add Property"** in the top-left dropdown.
2. Under **"URL prefix"**, enter:
   ```text
   https://hrlpavan.github.io/hrl-international-website-/
   ```
3. Click **Continue**.

### Step 3: Verify Domain Ownership
* **Method A (Instant via HTML Tag)**:
  1. Select **"HTML tag"** verification.
  2. Copy the `content="..."` key (e.g., `google-site-verification=XYZ123...`).
  3. Replace the placeholder in `index.html` or submit.
* **Method B (Instant via GitHub / Google Account)**:
  If your Google Account has Google Analytics or owns the domain, it verifies automatically with 1 click.

### Step 4: Submit Your XML Sitemap
1. In the left navigation menu, click **"Sitemaps"**.
2. Under **"Add a new sitemap"**, enter:
   ```text
   sitemap.xml
   ```
3. Click **Submit**.
4. Status will immediately show: **"Success"** with all pages discovered!

### Step 5: Request Instant Crawl (URL Inspection)
1. In the top search bar of Google Search Console, paste:
   `https://hrlpavan.github.io/hrl-international-website-/`
2. Press **Enter**.
3. Click **"Request Indexing"** (forces Googlebot to fetch and rank the live site within hours).
4. Repeat for: `https://hrlpavan.github.io/hrl-international-website-/showcase.html`.
