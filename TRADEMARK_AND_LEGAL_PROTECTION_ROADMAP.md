## 1. Legal Distinction: Trademark vs. Patent vs. Corporate Registration

To achieve 100% airtight legal ownership of your company name, logo, and technology under your personal name **Pavan Kumar Sadashiv**, here is how Intellectual Property Law is structured:

1. **Trademark (™ / ®)**:
   * **Protects**: The Brand Name (*HRL International*), the Full Corporate Name (*HRL International Private Limited*), the Red Typography Logo, and the Company Motto.
   * **Ownership**: Registered directly under **Pavan Kumar Sadashiv** (as Individual / Startup Proprietor).
   * **Duration**: 10 Years (Renewable indefinitely every 10 years).

2. **Corporate Name Reservation & Incorporation (MCA SPICe+)**:
   * **Protects**: Exclusive nationwide rights on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) registry preventing any other business in India from registering under *HRL International Private Limited*.
   * **Signatory / Director**: Pavan Kumar Sadashiv (DIN Holder).

3. **Software / Algorithm Patent (Form 1 - IPO)**:
   * **Protects**: Novel technical inventions, proprietary algorithms, and DaVinci Resolve OFX video plugin architectures.

4. **Copyright (©)**:
   * **Protects**: Source code, user interface designs, sound synthesis models, and video deliverables.

## 3. Step-by-Step Filing Process with Government of India (IP India)

### Step 1: Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
* Class 3 DSC issued for Pavan Kumar Sadashiv (required for e-filing on IP India portal `ipindiaonline.gov.in`).

### Step 2: Conduct Public Trademark Search
* Verify no conflicting marks on `ipindiaonline.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch` under Classes 42, 41, 35, and 9.

### Step 3: File Form TM-A (Application for Registration of Trademark)
* **Applicant Type**: Individual / Startup / Small Enterprise (50% Government Fee Concession: ₹4,500 per class instead of ₹9,000).
* **Proprietor Name**: Pavan Kumar Sadashiv.
* **Type of Mark**: Word Mark (`HRL International`) + Device Mark (Official Red HRL Logo).
* **User Date Affidavit**: Claim **August 2026** as the Date of First Commercial Use (supported by public timestamped GitHub releases and web deployments).

### Step 4: Use the ™ Symbol Immediately
* As soon as Form TM-A is submitted and the Application Number is issued, you are legally entitled to use the **`HRL International™`** symbol on all websites, letterheads, and software products.
* Once the Registrar issues the Registration Certificate (following publication in the Trade Marks Journal), you transition to the registered **`®`** symbol.
