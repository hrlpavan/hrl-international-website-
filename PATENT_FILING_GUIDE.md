## 2. Step-by-Step Indian Patent Office (IPO) e-Filing Procedure

1. **Portal Access**: Log in to the [IP India Online e-Filing Portal](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/epatentfiling/goitin/login.aspx) using your Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
2. **Mandatory Forms to Submit**:
   - **Form 1**: Application for Grant of Patent (Applicant: *HRL International Private Limited*, Inventor: *Pavan Kumar Sadashiv*).
   - **Form 2**: Complete Specification (Use exact contents from [`PATENT_SPECIFICATION_DOSSIER_RULE_BREAKING.md`](file:///Users/pavankumars/.gemini/antigravity/brain/57bb185e-4458-4af7-ae8c-1fe87eb23b85/PATENT_SPECIFICATION_DOSSIER_RULE_BREAKING.md)).
   - **Form 3**: Statement and Undertaking under Section 8.
   - **Form 5**: Declaration as to Inventorship (Attesting *Pavan Kumar Sadashiv* as the True and First Inventor).
   - **Form 9 / Form 18**: Request for Early Publication & Request for Accelerated Examination (under Startup India DIPP / DPIIT recognition for 80% fee reduction and fast-track grant within 6–12 months).
3. **Official Fee (Startup / Individual)**: ₹1,600 (Form 1 + Form 2) + ₹8,000 (Form 18 Expedited Examination).
