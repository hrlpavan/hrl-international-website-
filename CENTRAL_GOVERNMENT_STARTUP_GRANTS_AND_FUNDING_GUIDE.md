## 1. Top 4 Central Government Funding Schemes for HRL International

### A. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) — DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry
* **Grant & Funding Amount**: Up to **₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakhs Grant)** for Proof of Concept / Prototype + Up to **₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs Debt/Convertible Debentures)** for Market Commercialization.

* **Eligibility**: Early-stage tech startups with innovative software/media-tech solutions registered under 2 years.
* **Target Regional Incubators**: NITK Science & Technology Entrepreneurs Park (STEP) Surathkal / SCEM Incubation Hub / IIT Bombay.

## 2. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply on Central Govt Portals

### Step 1: Obtain DPIIT Startup Recognition (Startup India)
1. Visit **[https://www.startupindia.gov.in](https://www.startupindia.gov.in)**.
2. Click **"Register"** &rarr; Enter your founder details (Pavan Kumar Sadashiv) and company details (HRL International Private Limited).
3. Apply for **DPIIT Recognition Certificate** (Provides 80% discount on Patent fees, 50% discount on Trademark fees, and unlocks all Central Government grant schemes).

### Step 2: Apply for SISFS on the Central Seed Fund Portal
1. Visit **[https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in](https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in)**.
2. Click **"Apply Now"** &rarr; Log in with your Startup India credentials.
3. Select your preferred Central Incubator (e.g., **NITK Surathkal STEP** or **SCEM Hub**).
4. Fill in your project title, 2.5M+ traction, and funding requirement (₹20L – ₹50L).
5. Submit for screening.

### Step 3: Register on MeitY Startup Hub
1. Visit **[https://meitystartuphub.in](https://meitystartuphub.in)**.
2. Click **"Startups Register"**.
3. Link your AI/software products, GitHub repository (`github.com/hrlpavan`), and live showcase (`showcase.html`).
4. Apply to active MeitY TIDE 2.0 and GenAI innovation challenges.
