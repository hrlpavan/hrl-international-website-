## 5. Statutory Financial Governance & Compliance Standards

1. **Dedicated Escrow / Grant Bank Account**: All grant funds are maintained in a dedicated, unencumbered Current Account in a scheduled commercial bank in Mangaluru, Karnataka.
2. **Chartered Accountant (CA) Audit**: Bi-annual audits ensuring 100% adherence to line-item budget heads.
3. **Statutory Utilization Certificate (Form GFR 12-A)**: Issued by an independent statutory auditor upon completion of each milestone before tranche release.
4. **Zero Diversion Clause**: Funds will strictly not be utilized for speculative investments, personal expenses, or non-project expenditures.
